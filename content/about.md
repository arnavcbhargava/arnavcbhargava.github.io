# About Me

I just graduated with a MSc in Mathematics and Foundations of Computer Science with a Distinction and 81% overall.

I hold a First Class Honours from the University of Edinburgh as well.

During my time at Edinburgh, I served as the Vice-President of TypeSIG - the university's only CS theory-focused student society.

Theory underpins innovation, it balances nuance and rigour with wonder and beauty.

Outreach in theoretical computer science means a lot to me. In February 2025, I organized the Track A part of [**TUPLES 2025**](https://typesig.pl/tuples2025/), which is a student-run conference where researchers from across the UK gave talks aimed at students. It was a great way to make cutting-edge theory feel more accessible and inspiring.

## Research Overview

I am interested in learning theory and its connections to complexity, pseudo-randomness and explicit constructions of PRGs, extractors and expander graphs.

I also have a vested interest in algebraic topology.
